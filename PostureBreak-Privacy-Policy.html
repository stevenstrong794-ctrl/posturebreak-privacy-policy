PostureBreak Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

PostureBreak stores session history and preferences locally. This policy describes the app's current data practices and should be updated if they change.

Information stored on your device

PostureBreak stores posture-break session history and app preferences (such as focus duration, break duration, daily goal, notification preference, vibration preference, and posture prompt preference) in local Android app storage. This information is used to provide the timer, history, and progress features.

Notifications and vibration

If you enable reminders, the app may post local Android notifications when a focus session ends. If you enable haptic feedback, the app may use the device vibrator. Android notification permission is requested where required. These features do not require an account.

Advertising, analytics, and sharing

PostureBreak uses Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage consent choices where required. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the device's IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This information is encrypted in transit. Android advertising ID collection is optional and can be controlled by the user or app configuration.

The app requests ads only when UMP reports that ads can be requested. A privacy options entry point is shown in Settings when UMP requires it. The app does not send its local posture-break history to the ad SDK.

Data retention and deletion

Session history and preferences remain in the app's private local storage until you clear history inside Settings or uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for app data. Clearing history removes saved session records from the app database.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children or other users. The advertising SDK may process identifiers and interaction information for advertising. The publisher should review the selected AdMob settings and applicable requirements for the app's audience and distribution regions.
